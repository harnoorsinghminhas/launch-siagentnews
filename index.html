The three stamps

  • Confirmed

    The source itself says so: a company, a court, a government, a filing.

  • Reported · n outlets

    Credible outlets report it, but no primary source has confirmed it. We count the outlets.

  • Still open

    One outlet, thin sourcing, or the reports disagree. We say what would settle it.

A stamp can change. When it does, we say so on the story and in the corrections log.

About

A newsroom habit, applied to AI.

SI Agent News follows superintelligence (SI), the next generation of artificial intelligence (AI), and the rumors around it. It reads thousands of sources across 22 categories and holds each claim to the same test: who says it, how many say it, and what would settle it.

It is built by one person. AI does the reading and the first-pass stamp; a person sets the rules and reviews the stamps. Sources are credited on every story; where a claim has only one source, we say so.

SI = superintelligence, the new name for AI (artificial intelligence). Our wider network, including the daily brief and the SI Stylebook, lives at siagentsignal.com.
